PicsArt Review: Is Gold Worth It in 2026?

By AppKhazana Team · Updated October 21, 2026 · 6 min read

PicsArt has 1 billion+ downloads and a reputation as the Swiss-army knife of mobile photo editing — now with AI tools baked in. We tested both tiers to answer: does the free version survive, or is Gold the real app?

What you get free

  • Full basic editor — crop, filters, adjustments, 200+ fonts, millions of stickers
  • Collage maker & templates — social posts, stories, thumbnails
  • Limited AI tools — background remover and AI effects work, but on a weekly credit budget (around 5 generations)
  • Video editor included — basic clips, music, glitch effects

What Gold unlocks (~$55.99/year or monthly)

  • Unlimited AI — background remover, object eraser, AI image generator, no weekly cap
  • No ads, no watermarks — premium assets and AI generations export clean
  • Premium stickers, fonts & templates — the ones you actually see in trending edits
  • High-res exports — free exports can be resolution-limited on some tools

The honest catches

  • The free tier is an ad farm — frequent pop-ups interrupt the flow constantly
  • 3.9-star reality — 12M+ reviews average below 4 stars; the most common complaints are aggressive upselling and bugs
  • AI credits run out fast — if AI is why you came, the free tier won't last a weekend
  • Subscription pricing shifts often — always check the current price in-app before paying

Free alternatives for the same jobs

PicsArt featureFree alternativeOur take
Background removerremove.bg (web) / PhotoRoom free tierJust as good for one-off cuts
Basic photo editingSnapseed (fully free, no ads)Better editor, zero cost
Design templatesCanva free tierStronger for posts and thumbnails
AI image generationLeonardo / Ideogram free tiersMore control, separate app
Our verdict: PicsArt Free is fine for casual sticker-and-filter fun — but every serious tool is paywalled. Get Gold only if you edit 10+ photos a week and want everything in one app; the annual plan is reasonable value at ~$4.67/month. Everyone else: use Snapseed for editing and free web tools for background removal, and save your money.
