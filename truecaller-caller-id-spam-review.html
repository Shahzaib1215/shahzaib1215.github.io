Truecaller Review: The Best Spam Blocker — With a Privacy Catch

By AppKhazana Team · Updated October 21, 2026 · 7 min read

Truecaller names unknown callers better than anything else on Android — 500M+ users feed its crowdsourced database. In Pakistan, where spam and scam calls are constant, that's genuinely useful. But the same feature has a cost most reviews skip. Here's the full picture.

What the free tier does (ad-supported)

  • Real-time caller ID — see who's calling even if the number isn't saved
  • Spam flagging & blocking — auto-blocks telemarketers, robocallers, scammers
  • Reverse number lookup — search any number after the fact
  • SMS spam filtering — sorts scam texts into a spam folder

What Premium adds (~$2.99/month or ~$29.99/year, varies by region)

  • No ads — the free app is genuinely ad-heavy
  • AI Assistant — answers spam calls for you and sends a live transcript
  • Call recording with AI summaries (region-dependent)
  • Incognito mode + "who viewed my profile" — look numbers up without being seen

The part others skip: privacy

Truecaller builds its directory partly from your contact book. Your number can be searchable even if you've never installed the app — because someone who has you saved did. The company had a confirmed data breach in 2019 affecting hundreds of millions of users. If you install it, review Settings → Permissions before granting anything beyond the basics: the app works for caller ID without full contact access.

Our verdict: For raw spam-blocking power on Android, Truecaller Free wins — and you probably never need Premium. The free tier does the actual job. Pay only if the ads genuinely annoy you. But go in with open eyes: you're trading contact data for the directory. If that trade-off bothers you, use your phone's built-in spam protection or Hiya instead.
