5 Best Free PDF Reader Apps for Android (2026)

By AppKhazana Team · Updated October 27, 2026 · 7 min read

PDFs are everywhere — class notes, job applications, bank statements, government forms. Every Android phone opens a PDF somehow, but the built-in viewer usually stops at reading. We tested the most popular free PDF readers for one question: which one lets you read, highlight, fill forms and sign documents without pushing you into a subscription?

What we tested

  • Reading comfort — smooth scrolling, night mode, reflow for small screens
  • Annotation — highlight, underline, notes and freehand markup
  • Forms & signing — fillable forms, e-signatures, saving your changes
  • Honest free tier — what is genuinely free vs what sits behind a paywall

1. Xodo PDF Reader — best all-round free reader

Xodo covers almost everything in its free tier: fast rendering, highlight and annotation tools, form filling, e-signatures, and cloud sync with Google Drive and Dropbox. Its tabbed interface makes juggling several documents easy, and it handles large textbooks without stuttering. Some advanced tools (like redaction and OCR) need the paid tier, but for everyday reading and signing, the free version is hard to beat.

2. Adobe Acrobat Reader — best for official forms

Adobe's own reader is the safest bet when a form must render exactly as designed — government forms, bank paperwork and contracts. Reading, commenting and basic signing are free; editing text and converting files need a subscription. It is heavier than the alternatives, but for documents that must look perfect, it is the reference standard.

3. Foxit PDF Reader — best lightweight reader

Foxit opens fast, stays light on storage and battery, and still includes annotation, bookmarks and form filling. If your phone is low on space or you just want a PDF to open instantly, Foxit is the pragmatic pick.

4. Librera Reader — best open-source, ad-free reader

Librera is fully open-source with no ads and no in-app purchases at all. It reads PDFs and ebooks (EPUB, MOBI), supports night mode and bookmarks, and respects your privacy — no account, no tracking. Annotation is more basic than Xodo's, but for pure reading it is excellent.

5. Google Drive — best if you already have it

Before installing anything, check what you already have: Google Drive opens PDFs decently, and it is pre-installed on most Android phones. It cannot annotate or sign, but for a quick read of an attachment, it is already there — no install needed.
