5 Best Free Weather Apps for Android (2026)

By AppKhazana Team · Updated October 31, 2026 · 7 min read

Every phone ships with a weather widget, but most of them are vague — a sun icon and a temperature that is already wrong by noon. We compared the most popular free weather apps on a mid-range Android phone for one question: which one tells you what the weather will actually do, before you leave the house?

What we tested

  • Forecast usefulness — hourly detail, rain timing, and how far ahead it plans
  • Radar & alerts — live rain radar and severe-weather warnings that arrive in time
  • Ads & clutter — whether ads get in the way of a 10-second weather check
  • Everyday use — widgets, multiple locations, and how much battery it uses in the background

1. AccuWeather — best all-round free weather app

AccuWeather is the easiest recommendation for most people: clear hourly and daily forecasts, minute-by-minute rain timing for your exact location, and severe-weather alerts. The free tier is ad-supported, but the forecast itself is complete — nothing essential is locked behind a subscription. If you only install one weather app, install this one.

2. The Weather Channel — best radar and storm tracking

The Weather Channel's radar is the star here: animated rain maps, storm tracks and 24-hour future radar that show where rain is heading, not just where it is now. The free tier carries ads and pushes its premium tier, but for storm season it is the most informative free radar you will find.

3. Windy.com — best for weather nerds, and genuinely ad-free

Windy is the odd one out, and the best one for anyone who actually reads a weather map: dozens of map layers (wind, rain, temperature, waves), and — unusually — it lets you compare several professional forecast models for the same place instead of trusting one. Its listing says it is free with no ads, and that is rare in this category. The interface takes a day to learn, but nothing else comes close for detail.

4. 1Weather — best looking daily app

1Weather is the polished option: a clean daily forecast, a 10-day outlook, hourly rain chances, radar maps and plenty of customizable widgets. It is free with ads, and it is the one to pick if you want your home screen widget to look good as well as work.

5. Google Weather (Pixel) — best if you already have one

Pixel phones ship with Google's own Weather app, and it is better than most people realize: AI-summarized forecasts, precipitation maps, severe-weather warnings and a clean, ad-free design. It is Pixel-only, so check your phone first — if it is already installed, you may not need anything else on this list.

Our verdict: install AccuWeather first — it answers the daily question (do I need a jacket, will it rain at 5?) fastest. Add Windy.com alongside it if you ever check radar or plan around wind and rain. Skip any "free" weather app that demands heavy permissions or fills the screen with video ads before showing a temperature — a weather app has no business doing either.
