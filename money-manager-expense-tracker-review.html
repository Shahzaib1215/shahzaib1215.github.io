Money Manager (Realbyte) Review: The Best Free Expense Tracker in 2026?

By AppKhazana Team · Updated October 21, 2026 · 6 min read

Money Manager by Realbyte Inc. has quietly been one of the best-rated expense trackers on the Play Store for years (~4.7 stars). We tested it for daily use to answer one question: can it replace your notebook — or your bank's app — for tracking spending?

What it does well

  • Fast manual entry — add an expense in seconds with a built-in calculator and quick-add widgets on your home screen
  • Custom categories & sub-categories — split groceries from dining out, track exactly what you want
  • Real statistics — pie charts and trend graphs that show where your money actually goes each month
  • Budgets with alerts — set a monthly limit per category and get warned before you cross it
  • Multi-account + recurring entries — cash, bank, wallet, plus automatic monthly bills
  • Works offline — everything is stored on your phone; no bank login required, ever

The honest catches

  • No automatic bank sync in the free tier — every transaction is manual entry; cross-device sync costs extra (~$2.49/month)
  • Ads in the free version — not intrusive, but they exist
  • Dated design — it looks like an app from 2015, though it's fast
  • Excel export only in the paid version — free users can view, but not export, their data

Free vs paid

PlanWhat you getOur take
FreeFull tracking, budgets, stats, widgets — with adsEnough for 9 out of 10 people
Premium (one-time ~$5.99)No ads, backup, Excel exportA fair one-time deal — no subscription trap
Data sync (~$2.49/mo)Sync across phones/tabletsOnly if you track on two devices
Our verdict: If you want a no-nonsense expense tracker that respects your privacy (no bank login, no cloud account needed), Money Manager's free tier is the best option on Android. Pay the one-time premium only if ads bother you or you need Excel exports. Just know that bank SMS auto-tracking isn't its game — for that, look at apps built specifically for Pakistani banks.
