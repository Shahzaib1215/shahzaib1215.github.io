7 Free Photo Editors Better Than Paid Apps (2026)

By AppKhazana Team · Updated October 20, 2026 · 7 min read

Phone cameras keep getting better, but the editing app you use matters more than the camera. We re-tested the classics and the 2026 newcomers with the same 5 photos: a portrait, a landscape, a low-light shot, a food photo, and one that needed object removal. Here is the honest ranking.

1. Snapseed — Still the king

Google's Snapseed remains the most powerful completely free editor: selective adjustments, healing, curves, and RAW support with zero ads and zero paywall. Its interface looks dated, but nothing free touches its precision.

Best for: Serious editing without spending a rupee.

⬇️ Download Snapseed (official Play Store)

2. Lightroom Mobile (Free tier) — Best presets

Adobe's free tier gives you excellent presets, light/color controls, and cloud sync for a few photos. Selective edits and healing need Premium — but for quick, great-looking edits the free version is plenty.

Best for: Instagram-ready looks in one tap.

⬇️ Download Lightroom (official Play Store)

3. Picsart (Free tier) — Best creative tools

Stickers, AI backgrounds, collage maker, and a massive community. The free version is ad-heavy and many premium assets are locked, but the creative toolkit is unmatched for fun edits.

Best for: Creative, playful edits and social posts.

⬇️ Download Picsart (official Play Store)

4. Pixlr — Best browser-grade editor on mobile

Pixlr packs layers, blending, and AI cutout into a free app. Slightly cluttered interface, but the feature list embarrasses apps that charge monthly.

Best for: Layer-based editing on the go.

⬇️ Download Pixlr (official Play Store)

5. Remini (Free tier) — Best photo enhancer

For blurry old photos, Remini's AI enhancement is genuinely impressive. Free tier is limited to a few enhances per day with ads — but for rescuing one precious photo, it works.

Best for: Fixing blurry or old photos.

⬇️ Download Remini (official Play Store)

6. PhotoDirector (Free tier) — Best AI tools

AI sky replacement, object removal, and cartoon effects. The free tier watermarks some AI exports, but the tools themselves are fun to try.

Best for: AI-powered creative edits.

⬇️ Download PhotoDirector (official Play Store)

7. Google Photos editor — Best built-in option

Already on your phone: surprisingly capable auto-enhance, portrait blur, and Magic Eraser (on supported devices). Zero downloads needed.

Best for: Quick fixes without installing anything.
